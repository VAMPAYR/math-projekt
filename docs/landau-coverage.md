# Landau preparation: scope and remaining work

The [Landau Institute chair](https://chair.itp.ac.ru/index.php?lang=en&sub=minimum) describes eleven examinations in mathematics and theoretical physics. This project provides original written learning material and practice; it does not administer an oral examination or certify readiness for the official examinations. The subject names below map the preparation material to that scope, not to a promise of exhaustive syllabus coverage.

| Official subject | Preparation in this app | Further depth needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics I | Operations and proof; complex numbers, linear algebra, ODEs, vector and tensor calculus | More coordinate/index calculations, rigorous existence and uniqueness, and general-domain boundary problems |
| Mechanics | Newtonian models, conservation, oscillations, action, Hamiltonian mechanics and advanced analytical methods | More constrained systems, canonical transformations, nonlinear dynamics and challenging derivations |
| Field theory | Electromagnetism, relativity, field actions and gravity foundations | Covariant tensor derivations, gauge fixing, radiation and broader relativistic field equations |
| Mathematics II | Analytic functions, contours and residues, transforms, asymptotics and special functions | Analytic continuation, branch-cut contours, controlled error estimates and broader boundary-value applications |
| Quantum mechanics | States and observables, dynamics, spin, oscillators, perturbation, scattering and quantum methods | More spectral theory, angular-momentum algebra, central potentials, WKB and multistep problems |
| Quantum electrodynamics | Relativistic field motivation, scattering, propagator and regulated-model examples | Full canonical quantization, loop calculations, gauge identities and spin/phase-space calculations |
| Statistical physics I | Entropy, ensembles, thermodynamic quantities and quantum statistics | More derivations of potentials, response functions, phase equilibrium and fluctuations |
| Continuum mechanics | Conservation, fluids, elasticity, waves and viscous flow | Deeper constitutive laws, stability, boundary layers and nonlinear materials |
| Electrodynamics of continua | Material boundary conditions, anisotropy and dispersive-response models | More material boundary-value problems, magnetized media, radiation in media and superconductivity |
| Statistical physics II | Quantum distributions, phase transitions, mean-field models and fluctuations | Broader many-body methods, correlations, critical behavior and quantum matter |
| Physical kinetics | Transport, diffusion, relaxation and kinetic-theory bridges | More detailed collision integrals, transport coefficients, kinetic boundary problems and plasma applications |

The route in the app links prerequisites to later lessons in both directions. Use a worked example to learn a method, solve the independent variation without looking at its solution, and check the assumptions and units. Familiarity with the examples does not by itself establish examination-level mastery.
