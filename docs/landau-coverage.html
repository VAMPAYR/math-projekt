Landau preparation: scope and remaining work

The Landau Institute chair describes eleven examinations in mathematics and theoretical physics. These original written lessons and practice do not administer an oral examination or certify examination readiness.

The subject names map the available preparation to the official scope. Further depth remains necessary in every area.

Mathematics I

Available: Operations and proof; complex numbers, linear algebra, ODEs, vector and tensor calculus

Further depth: More coordinate/index calculations, rigorous existence and uniqueness, and general-domain boundary problems

Mechanics

Available: Newtonian models, conservation, oscillations, action, Hamiltonian mechanics and advanced analytical methods

Further depth: More constrained systems, canonical transformations, nonlinear dynamics and challenging derivations

Field theory

Available: Electromagnetism, relativity, field actions and gravity foundations

Further depth: Covariant tensor derivations, gauge fixing, radiation and broader relativistic field equations

Mathematics II

Available: Analytic functions, contours and residues, transforms, asymptotics and special functions

Further depth: Analytic continuation, branch-cut contours, controlled error estimates and broader boundary-value applications

Quantum mechanics

Available: States and observables, dynamics, spin, oscillators, perturbation, scattering and quantum methods

Further depth: More spectral theory, angular-momentum algebra, central potentials, WKB and multistep problems

Quantum electrodynamics

Available: Relativistic field motivation, scattering, propagator and regulated-model examples

Further depth: Full canonical quantization, loop calculations, gauge identities and spin/phase-space calculations

Statistical physics I

Available: Entropy, ensembles, thermodynamic quantities and quantum statistics

Further depth: More derivations of potentials, response functions, phase equilibrium and fluctuations

Continuum mechanics

Available: Conservation, fluids, elasticity, waves and viscous flow

Further depth: Deeper constitutive laws, stability, boundary layers and nonlinear materials

Electrodynamics of continua

Available: Material boundary conditions, anisotropy and dispersive-response models

Further depth: More material boundary-value problems, magnetized media, radiation in media and superconductivity

Statistical physics II

Available: Quantum distributions, phase transitions, mean-field models and fluctuations

Further depth: Broader many-body methods, correlations, critical behavior and quantum matter

Physical kinetics

Available: Transport, diffusion, relaxation and kinetic-theory bridges

Further depth: More detailed collision integrals, transport coefficients, kinetic boundary problems and plasma applications

Use a worked example to learn a method, solve the independent variation before viewing its solution, then check assumptions and units. Familiarity with examples alone does not establish examination-level mastery.
